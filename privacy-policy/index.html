Privacy Policy

MassClassMedia · Effective 17 September 2026

MassClassMedia is operated by a single individual (“we”, “us”). This policy covers MassClassMedia, a private internal tool used to upload and post our own video clips to social media accounts that we ourselves own and control.

The tool is not offered to the public. There are no user accounts, no sign-ups, and no customers. The only accounts that authorize it are our own.

What the tool collects

When one of our own social media accounts authorizes the tool, we receive and store:

  • An account identifier (for example, a TikTok open ID) — used to make certain a clip is uploaded to the correct one of our channels.
  • The account display name — shown to the operator as a confirmation of which account was authorized.
  • Access and refresh tokens — the credentials that permit the upload or post.

That is the complete list. We do not collect followers, contacts, messages, comments, viewing history, analytics, location, or any information about anyone other than the account holder, who is us.

Where it is stored

All of the above is stored locally, in files on the operator's own computer. There is no MassClassMedia server, no database, and no cloud account holding this data.

What we do with it

Solely to deliver video files we produced ourselves to our own accounts: either as drafts for a human to finish in the platform's app, or as direct posts confirmed by a human on the tool's posting screen. Nothing else.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share this data with anyone.
  • We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or tracking.
  • We do not access any account other than our own.
  • We do not publish anything without a human choosing the audience and settings and confirming that post.

How long we keep it

Tokens and account identifiers are kept only while the authorization is active. Revoking the tool's access from within the platform's own settings ends its ability to act, and the stored credentials are deleted.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs of requests; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children

This tool is not directed to children and is not usable by anyone other than its operator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
