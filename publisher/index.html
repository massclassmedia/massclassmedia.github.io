The MassClassMedia publisher

An internal tool that moves finished clips from our production machine into our own social media accounts. One operator, our accounts only.

Not a public product. There are no sign-ups, no customers and no third-party accounts. The only accounts that authorize the publisher are the MassClassMedia channels listed on the Channels page.

What it does

Once a clip has been rendered and approved, the publisher delivers it to each platform. Depending on the platform and the clip, that means one of two things:

  • Upload as a draft. The clip lands in the account's own inbox inside the platform's app, where a person finishes the post by hand.
  • Direct post from the posting screen. A person opens the publisher's posting screen, chooses the audience and settings for that clip, and confirms. The publisher then posts it to that account.

Either way, a human makes the final call on every post. The publisher never posts on its own.

How a post works, step by step

  1. Sign in once. The operator signs into one of our own accounts through the platform's official login. The publisher records which account authorized it so clips can never go to the wrong channel.
  2. Pick a clip. Approved clips appear in a queue with their title and caption.
  3. Choose the settings. On the posting screen the operator selects who can see the post, whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, and whether the post contains any commercial content. Nothing is pre-selected. The Post button stays disabled until every required choice has been made.
  4. Confirm. The operator presses Post. The clip is transferred to the platform and the result is shown on screen.

TikTok integration

On TikTok the publisher is built on TikTok for Developers, using these products and permissions:

Product / scopeWhat it is used for
Login Kit
user.info.basic, user.info.profile		 Sign in to one of our own accounts and read its ID and display name, so the publisher can confirm which of our channels it is connected to.
Content Posting API
video.upload		 Upload a clip to that account's inbox as a draft, for a person to finish in the TikTok app.
Content Posting API
video.publish		 Post a clip directly from the posting screen, after a person has chosen the audience and interaction settings for that post.

The posting screen follows TikTok's content-sharing guidelines: the privacy level is loaded from the account's own settings with no default, comment/duet/stitch are off until turned on and greyed out where the account disallows them, commercial content must be declared before posting, and the music usage confirmation is shown before the Post button becomes active.

What it stores

Only what it needs to act on our own accounts: an account identifier, the account's display name, and the access tokens the platform issues. All of it stays in files on the operator's own computer. There is no MassClassMedia server and no database. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Where it runs

The publisher runs on a single production computer. This website exists to host the tool's public identity: its name, logo, privacy policy, terms of service and the sign-in return page that hands an authorization back to the tool.
