MassClassMedia

Independent fan clip channels covering political livestreams, and the in-house tool that publishes them.

What we do

MassClassMedia produces short-form clips from publicly broadcast political livestreams and posts them to its own social media channels on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. Every channel identifies itself as a fan channel and is not affiliated with the creators whose streams it references.

Clips are short, focused on a single point the host is making, captioned, and released on a daily schedule. Reference material the host cites on stream is shown on screen so viewers can follow the argument.

The MassClassMedia publisher

MassClassMedia is also the name of our internal publishing tool. It moves finished clips from our own production machine into our own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public: the only accounts that ever authorize it are the ones listed above, and a person on our side confirms every post.

Content standards

  • Every channel is labelled as a fan channel in its name, bio and description.
  • Clips credit the host and link to their live stream.
  • Removal requests from the creators we clip are honored on receipt.
  • Nothing is posted without a person reviewing it first.
