MassClassMedia

Independent fan clip channels covering political livestreams.

MassClassMedia produces short-form clips from publicly broadcast political livestreams and posts them to its own social media channels. All channels identify themselves as fan channels and are not affiliated with the creators whose streams they reference.

MassClassMedia is also the name of our internal publishing tool, which moves finished clips from our own production machine into our own social media accounts for a human to review and post.
