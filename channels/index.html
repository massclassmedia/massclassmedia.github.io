How clips are made

  1. Source. Streams are publicly broadcast on Twitch. Both hosts have said on stream that they welcome their content being reused to spread the message.
  2. Selection. A clip has to make one clear political point. Banter without a point is not shipped.
  3. Edit. Silence is cut, the speaker is framed, captions are added, and any article the host references is shown on screen for a few seconds.
  4. Review. A person approves each clip before it is scheduled.
  5. Release. Clips go out on a fixed daily schedule to every platform above.
